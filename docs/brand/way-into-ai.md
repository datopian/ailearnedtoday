# Way Into AI

## Offer

Way Into AI helps people get oriented and act well in a fast-moving field. It combines practical ways to use AI with clear-eyed context about what AI is changing.

## Audience

Curious, capable generalists: people who want to use AI in their work and life, and understand its implications without needing to already be AI insiders.

## Voice

Direct, useful, and unpretentious. Prefer concrete examples and honest uncertainty to hype, jargon, or false certainty. Explain enough to make a reader more capable, then point them to the next useful thing.

## Two routes, one site

- **Use AI** is for guides, workflows, tools, and agentic coding.
- **Understand AI** is for capability, governance, work, power, safety, and the broader consequences of AI.

The routes answer different reader needs, but share one editorial home. Posts and logs are the chronological record; these routes are the way in.
