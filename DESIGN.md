# Design: Way Into AI

The homepage is a field dispatch: practical, direct, and editorial rather than glossy or futuristic. Its job is to make one connected offer legible at a glance: use AI well, and understand the changes it brings.

The visual system uses warm paper (`#f8f1e2`), bottle-green ink (`#173d33`), pale field green, and a clay-red signal. The navigation is a simple wordmark. The two doors are differentiated by direct language and two field-colour surfaces, not by decorative illustration.

`index.md` uses Flowershow Markdown mode with standard HTML only, while `custom.css` owns the layout and responsive behaviour. The homepage occupies the page directly—no inset card, shadowed mockup, or generated hero art. Keep future additions semantic, source-grounded, and calm; avoid invented proof points, generic AI imagery, and competing calls to action.
