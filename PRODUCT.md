# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Curious, capable generalists who want to use AI in their work and life, and understand its wider consequences without already being AI insiders. They arrive needing a credible next step, not an exhaustive field guide.

## Product Purpose

Way Into AI helps people get oriented and act well in a fast-moving field. It offers two routes through the same editorial home: practical application and clear-eyed understanding.

## Positioning

Rather than making visitors choose between a tools-and-tips site and a policy/intellectual analysis site, Way Into AI gives both needs a legible, equal entrance and keeps the resulting work in one living record.

## Operating Context

Visitors enter through **Use AI** for guides, workflows, tools, and agentic coding, or **Understand AI** for capability, governance, work, power, safety, and consequences. Posts and logs are the chronological archive; the two routes are the orientation layer.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The existing site is Flowershow content authored in Markdown, with standard HTML and custom CSS available for the homepage. Preserve real routes and editorial material; do not invent testimonials, metrics, customers, or policy claims. The custom visual landing page must remain readable, responsive, and accessible.

## Brand Commitments

The name is Way Into AI. Its voice is direct, useful, and unpretentious: concrete examples and honest uncertainty over hype, jargon, or false certainty. The navigation uses a simple wordmark rather than a decorative logo. The two entry routes are named **Use AI** and **Understand AI**.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains practical guide and workflow material under `ref/` and `posts/`, contextual/policy material under the same editorial system, a two-tone logo at `assets/ailearnedtoday-logo.webp`, and several existing editorial images. No customer proof, quantitative performance claims, or dedicated photographic brand library has been supplied.

## Product Principles

- Give a first-time visitor an immediately useful choice.
- Keep practical agency and wider understanding equally serious.
- Make the site feel like a well-kept field notebook, not an AI growth funnel.
- Use editorial specificity and real material instead of generic AI imagery or claims.
- Let the archive grow without making it the burden of the first screen.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic headings, descriptive alternative text, keyboard-accessible links, readable contrast, and layouts that work at narrow mobile widths.
